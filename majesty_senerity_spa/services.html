Walk-In Services

Experience our range of walk-in services without the need for an appointment. Perfect for those who want to unwind and rejuvenate at their convenience.

Our walk-in services are available daily from 9 AM to 8 PM.

Walk-in services are subject to availability and are on a first-come, first-served basis.

Our walk-in services include:

  • Basic Massage
  • Facial Treatments
  • Nail Care

Please note that our walk-in services are limited due to their spontaneous nature.

Individual appointments are not available for walk-in services.If you would like a personalized treatment, please book an appointment in advance, as availability cannot be guaranteed.
