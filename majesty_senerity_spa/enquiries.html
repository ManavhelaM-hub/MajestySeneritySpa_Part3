    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    Here are some common questions and answers to help you with your enquiries:

    1. What services do you offer?

    We offer a wide range of services including Swedish massages, deep tissue massages, hot stone massages, facials, body treatments, and manicures and pedicures.

    2. How do I book an appointment?

    You can book an appointment by visiting our website or by calling us at +27 83 456 7890.

    3. What are your operating hours?

    Our spa is open from Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.

    4. What is your cancellation policy?

    Please contact us at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

    5. Do you offer gift vouchers?

    Yes, we offer gift vouchers for our services. Please contact us for more information on purchasing gift vouchers.

    6. Are your therapists certified?

    Yes, all of our therapists are certified and trained professionals with extensive experience in providing spa treatments.

    7. What should I wear during my spa treatment?

    We provide comfortable, loose-fitting clothing for our clients to change into before their treatments. You are welcome to bring your own comfortable attire if you prefer.
